🌻 Barnstorm: Harvest Rush

A farm track that snakes across the field and loops home down the return lane. You plant, water and harvest — no rent, no title deeds. Crops ripen with the weather, livestock pay out every dawn, and you sweep up ripe fields just by driving over them.

Pick your farmers — tap to join, tap the badge to switch Human / CPU

Season goal

Runs to the barn

2–4 farmers. Play solo against the CPU or pass-and-play together.

🌱 Plant a crop

📖 How to play

Barnstorm is a farming race. Move along the snaking track, work the land you stop on, and build the richest farm before someone finishes their runs to the barn.

🎲 Your turn
  • Roll the die and hop along the serpentine track — 49 plots winding up the field.
  • Roll a 6 and you get another roll (twice max).
  • Then you work the tile you landed on.
  • The track is a closed loop: tile 49 is the barn, and the return lane carries you home to the farmyard — so you pass your own fields again and again.
🚜 Gathering on the move
  • Driving straight over one of your own ripe crops brings it in without stopping — plant ahead of yourself and sweep the lot on a later lap.
  • Crossing The Big Barn pays +120 and banks a run — but only if you have covered a full lap of ground since your last one, so being blown backwards past it by a twister earns a consolation +40, not a free run.
  • Rolling back through The Farmyard pays +25 — and stopping there for a meal pays another +15, while the dog, the cat and the chickens all come out to see you.
  • Rivals' crops are safe unless you land on them.
🌱 Plots & crops
  • Land on empty soil → pay coins to plant one of three offered crops.
  • Land on your ripe crop → harvest it for full value.
  • Land on your young crop → water it, +1 day of growth.
  • Land on a rival's ripe crop → scrump it: you take 40%, they keep 60% — or be neighbourly and take a 25 coin tip instead.
  • Land on a rival's young crop → lend a hand for a 15 coin tip.
⚙️ The Old Mill
  • A real windmill stands on tile 25, dead centre of the field, and its sails turn with the weather — lazy in a drought, racing in a storm.
  • Land on it and it grinds every ripe crop you own into flour at +60% — the best price anywhere on the farm, and better than the market.
  • Nothing ripe? Sweep the millstones for the miller and take +40.
  • It sits at the hardest spot on the track to hit on purpose, so plan a lap around it.
☀️ Weather & days
  • A day passes when every farmer has had a turn.
  • Each dawn, crops grow by the weather: sun +1, rain +2, drought 0, storm +1 (and one crop may be flattened), rainbow +2 and richer harvests.
  • The forecast in the topbar shows tomorrow's sky.
🐄 Livestock
  • Coop tiles let you buy an animal.
  • Every animal pays coins every dawn, forever — a farm engine.
  • Chicken 🐔 · Sheep 🐑 · Pig 🐖 · Cow 🐄 — each louder and richer than the last.
🔊 The farmyard
  • Eighteen animals have a voice, and they answer to everything — dawn, harvests, purchases, tractors, twisters and quiet moments in between.
  • Every call is placed: you hear the cow where the cow is standing, and the goose on the far verge sounds like it is on the far verge.
  • Most calls get answered from the other side of the field a beat later.
  • Dawn is a proper dawn chorus, and the whole yard kicks off on a barn run.
  • A storm winds the animals up; rain and drought settle them down.
  • M mutes everything; 🎵 toggles just the music.
🪨 Soil quality
  • ✨ Rich soil — harvests worth 50% more.
  • 🪨 Rocky soil — crops need one extra day.
  • Plain soil is just plain soil.
🔔 Ending the season
  • 🌾 Barn Runs: you pick how many runs to the barn it takes — anywhere from 1 for a sprint to 6 for an epic. The first farmer to finish them all calls the Final Day. Everyone else gets one last turn, then every standing crop goes to market (ripe at full price, young at half of what it has grown), herds are sold off, and the richest farm wins.
  • ⏱️ Quick Season: ten days flat, then the same count-up. A short game.
  • 🪙 Harvest Tycoon: no clock at all — first farmer to bank 1,400 coins wins outright.
